# Mission Reflection

This activity gave me a better understanding of how AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud approach cloud computing. Although the three platforms provide many similar services, they have different strengths and are designed to support different types of users and workloads. I found AWS interesting because of the wide variety of services available through one cloud platform.

The three providers have several similarities. They all offer virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and other services that allow organizations to build applications without depending entirely on physical infrastructure. However, their tools and features differ depending on the type of workload being developed.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud provider because there is no single platform that is perfect for every organization. A company should consider its existing technology, budget, employees, application requirements, expected growth, security needs, and future plans. For example, a university that already relies heavily on Microsoft products may find Azure more convenient, while an organization working with data and artificial intelligence may benefit from Google Cloud.

Working as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that recommending a cloud platform involves more than simply choosing the most popular provider. The recommendation should match the customer's actual situation and technical requirements.
